# AttuneRadii migration record

AttuneRadii was the Python predecessor that established the small typed
relation grammar, depth-seven exhaustive search, repository signatures, and
the first MUI/Vue/Darkreader measurements. Its imported working tree was used
as migration evidence and then removed from permanent HEAD after the Flix and
Bazel implementations passed their parity laws.

The archive checkpoint contains the exact imported Python, tests, Grit, Rust,
Nix pins, locks, and 7,127-line detailed experimental specification. It is the
place to inspect historical implementation details. Current HEAD keeps the
resulting permanent concepts instead of a second runnable system.

## What survived

- Six directed Atlas atoms and a richer Radii algebra.
- Exhaustive enumeration of every well-typed composition through depth seven.
- Exact Datalog/physical parity.
- Source-independent repository signatures: extinction, expansion, reach,
  recurrence, and physical reuse.
- The law that native bitmap serialization is physical representation, never
  semantic identity.
- Content-addressed reuse around effectful acquisition and pure computation.

Current implementations are in `src/Radii.flix`, `src/Atlas.flix`,
`src/Repository/Structure.flix`, and `src/Repository/Physical.flix`. The full
SWE-Explore census protocol and evidence are under
`experiments/atlas-swe-explore/`.

## Why the imported tree was deleted

The Python/Nix tree was useful as a precise oracle, not as permanent runtime
architecture. Keeping it would preserve duplicate dependency graphs, a Nix
runtime route, a Python scientific implementation, and historical experiment
entry points that Bazel and Flix have replaced. Jujutsu history and frozen
Parquet evidence retain reproducibility without making readers understand two
systems.
